# Project Vision

## Problem

Independent creators — artists, writers, musicians, makers — have to choose between large platforms that take a significant cut of every sale or self-hosted solutions that require too much technical setup. There is no simple, low-friction way for an individual creator to put up a storefront, list a few products, and start selling directly to their audience.

## Intended Users

Individual creators (digital artists, photographers, musicians, writers, indie makers) who want to sell a small catalog of products — digital downloads or physical goods — directly to their audience without signing up for a large marketplace.

## Goals

- Let creators set up a public-facing profile page with their name and bio
- Let creators post products with a title, description, price, and image
- Let buyers browse a creator's storefront and complete a purchase via Stripe
- Keep the experience simple enough that a non-technical creator can use it

## Why This Project

Most creator tools either lock you into a large platform ecosystem or require significant configuration to self-host. DropShelf explores the middle ground: a purpose-built app that is simple by design, connects directly to Stripe, and gives creators a clean direct-to-consumer experience. This project connects to my interest in e-commerce, independent creator workflows, and payment integrations.

## Initial Scope

**In the first version:**
- Creator profile page (name, bio, profile image)
- Product listing page (title, description, price, image)
- Stripe Checkout integration for buying a listed product

**Deferred to later sprints:**
- Creator authentication and account management
- Order history and fulfillment tracking
- Multiple storefronts or team accounts
- Digital file delivery after purchase
- Search or discovery features across creators
