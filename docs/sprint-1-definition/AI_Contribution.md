# AI Contribution — Sprint 1

AI (Claude) contributed to Sprint 1 in two main ways: brainstorming and documentation drafting.

During the idea phase, I described a general concept — a creator selling platform — and used AI to pressure-test the scope. AI helped identify what was too broad for an eight-week project (multi-creator discovery, digital file delivery, full auth systems) and suggested cutting down to a single-creator storefront as the first version. That scope reduction was useful and I kept it.

For the Sprint 1 artifacts, I provided the project name, stack choice (Next.js full-stack), core features (creator profile, product listings, Stripe checkout), and my own reasoning for why the project matters. AI used those inputs to draft the initial content for `project-vision.md`, `requirements.md`, `architecture.md`, and `README.md`. I reviewed each file, confirmed the decisions reflected my intent, and accepted the drafts as accurate representations of my project direction.

All final decisions — what to build, what to cut, which stack to use, and why the project is meaningful to me — were mine. AI helped me get the words down faster and organized, but did not choose the project or determine its direction.
