2. Initial Understanding
DropShelf is an independent online store that lets creators sell physical and digital goods directly to their audience, giving them full control without a marketplace middleman.

Key Features Currently Implemented:

For Customers: Browse the storefront, purchase items securely via Stripe, and view your order history.
For Creators (Admins): Secure login (via Clerk), manage inventory (create, edit, or archive products), and process refunds or cancellations via Stripe.
Behind the Scenes: All product and order information is reliably saved in a database (Postgres).
